1. TERM OF LEASE AND LOCK-IN PERIOD
1.1 The lease is granted for an initial fixed term of 11 (eleven) months commencing
from January 1, 2026 and expiring on November 30, 2026.
1.2 Both parties agree to a mandatory Lock-in Period of 6 (six) months from the
Commencement Date. Neither party shall terminate this Agreement during the lock-in period.
If the Lessee vacates prior to the expiry of the lock-in period, the Lessee shall forfeit
the entire security deposit.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS

2.2 Payment shall be deposited electronically into designated Bank Account:
Bank: [REDACTED - BANK NAME]
Account Number: [REDACTED - ACCOUNT NUMBER]
IFSC/SWIFT: [REDACTED - ROUTING CODE]
2.3 Any delayed remittance beyond the 5th of the month shall attract a late interest charge
of 2.0% per month on the overdue amount.

3. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 Upon execution of this Agreement, the Lessee has deposited an Interest-Free
Refundable Security Deposit of $6,600.00 (Six Thousand Six Hundred Dollars), equivalent
to 3 (three) months' rent.
3.2 The Security Deposit shall be refunded by the Lessor to the Lessee within 15 (fifteen)
calendar days of the Lessee peacefully handing over vacant physical possession of the Demised
Premises, subject to legitimate deductions for unpaid utility dues or verified structural damage.

4. MAINTENANCE, REPAIRS, AND UTILITY CHARGES
4.1 The Lessee shall bear and promptly pay monthly society maintenance dues of $150.00,
water consumption charges, and electricity charges as per actual meter readings.
4.2 Routine day-to-day minor repairs (up to $50.00 per incident) such as bulb replacements,
tap washers, and minor plumbing shall be borne by the Lessee.
4.3 Major structural repairs, seepage remediation, and primary plumbing/electrical failure
not attributable to Lessee negligence shall be the responsibility of the Lessor.

5. RESTRICTIONS ON USE AND SUBLETTING PROHIBITION
5.1 The Demised Premises shall be utilized exclusively for private residential dwelling purposes
and for no other commercial, industrial, or unlawful activity.
5.2 The Lessee shall strictly not assign, transfer, license, or sublet the Demised Premises
or any part thereof to any third party under any circumstances. Any breach of this clause
shall constitute an incurable default entitling the Lessor to immediate eviction.
5.3 Hazardous materials, firearms, and illegal substances are strictly prohibited on the premises.

6. INSPECTION AND RIGHT OF ENTRY
6.1 The Lessor or their authorized representative reserves the right to inspect the Demised
Premises upon giving at least 24 (twenty-four) hours prior written or electronic notice,
during reasonable daytime hours (10:00 AM to 6:00 PM).

7. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
7.1 Post the completion of the 6-month Lock-in Period, either party may terminate this Agreement
by serving 1 (one) month advance written notice to the other party.
7.2 Failure by the Lessee to pay rent for two consecutive months shall entitle the Lessor
to immediately terminate this Agreement and re-enter the premises.

8. VACATING AND RESTORATION
8.1 Upon termination or expiry, the Lessee shall surrender the Demised Premises in the same
sound clean state and order as received, reasonable wear and tear excepted.
8.2 Deep cleaning costs and repainting charges (capped at $350.00) may be deducted from
the Security Deposit if the premises are handed over with marked walls or damage.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State.
9.2 Any dispute arising hereunder shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed mutually
by both parties, with the seat of arbitration situated at Metro City.
